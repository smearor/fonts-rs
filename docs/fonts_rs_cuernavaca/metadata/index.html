Glyph metadata: chess piece categories and style ordering.

Provides category lookup for Cuernavaca glyphs: every piece glyph belongs to king, queen, rook, bishop, knight, or pawn. The upside-down pieces keep their piece category - cuernavaca-a (white upside-down bishop) is a bishop, cuernavaca-d/f (rook+knight fairy, used as upside-down rook) is a rook. Square and diagram-border glyphs carry no category.

GlyphOrdering groups the pieces into four styles and links the non-base styles onto the light normal base:

  • light normal - white pieces, upright (cuernavaca-k .. p)
  • dark normal - black pieces, upright (cuernavaca-l .. o)
  • light upside-down - white pieces, rotated (cuernavaca-a/d/g)
  • dark upside-down - black pieces, rotated (cuernavaca-f/h/s)

Semantically the rotated glyphs are fairy pieces in chess-problem convention - a bishop+knight (archbishop), a rook+knight (chancellor), and a generic fairy knight (nightrider) - which is why only bishop, knight, and rook exist upside-down: king, queen, and pawn carry no canonical fairy role.

The link powers the detail sidebar’s Variants section, sorts each variant next to its base, and collapses the variants behind the base in the browse grid.

Categories are generated at build time from the glyph names - Cuernavaca ships no upstream keyword, category, or alias source, so the keyword and alias maps stay empty.

This module is only available when the metadata feature is enabled.
